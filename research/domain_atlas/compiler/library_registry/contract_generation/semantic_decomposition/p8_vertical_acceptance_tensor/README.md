# P8 product × vertical-slot × acceptance-class tensor

Every retained product requires acceptance in two unrelated verticals. Each vertical must pass eight
case-specific gate classes. The naive surface is therefore:

```text
59 products × 2 unrelated vertical slots × 8 acceptance classes = 944 obligations
```

P8 preserves all 944 exact obligations while factoring shared gate methods and evidence schemas into
eight workstreams. It emits 118 product/slot dockets, 118 empty acceptance-intake templates and one
two-unrelated-vertical refusal gate per product.

The four existing deterministic vertical compositions are structural candidates only. They create
45 product-to-composition relations and cover 14 products; 12 products appear in at least two
unrelated industry pilots. No composition is assigned to a slot, no gate receipt exists and no
vertical is accepted. Structural reuse, provider qualification, metric improvement and product
presence in a composition are never acceptance.

Workstream assets may share a question pattern, test method, evidence schema and negative-twin
design. Every exact product/vertical slot retains its own source cut, meaning, population, method,
physical occurrence, operational envelope, authority, effects, outcomes, change path, receipts and
verdict.

Run:

```sh
python3 build_p8.py
python3 validate.py
```
