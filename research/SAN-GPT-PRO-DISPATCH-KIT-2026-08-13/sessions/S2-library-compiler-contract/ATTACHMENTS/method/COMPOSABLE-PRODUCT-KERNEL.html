Edition 3 source-authority and separation ruling

The old SAN toolkit is deprecated evidence, explicitly including toolkit/factory/**, toolkit/foundation/**, old chassis/kernel/platform families and their local descriptors, registries, compilers, IRs and envelopes. This architecture MUST NOT import, wrap, extend or place canonical code in those trees. Recovered semantics require a symbol-level disposition and fresh proof.

The composition architecture has five separately owned boundaries, not one god-compiler: (0) a pure constitutional metamodel, (1) a Constitution Compiler, (2) an application compiler/linker, (3) obligation and coverage auditors, and (4) a deployment binder. The 24 concern axes are review lenses over explicit obligations. They are neither domain boundaries, mandatory fields, compiler passes nor an executable weave. A lens may reveal a missing contract; it may not mutate an artifact graph.

0. The correction

0.1 The question that was not being asked

YAML is irrelevant to the architectural problem

YAML, Rust syntax, a visual editor and an API are authoring carriers. Choosing among them answers nothing. The root methodological error of the earlier work was designing a grammar before enumerating everything the grammar had to cover — and declaring "capability packs plus an IR" while leaving the module system, composition semantics, decision algebra, runtime linking and recursive product structure undefined repeats that error one level up.

WHAT "A BAG OF LIBRARIES" LEAVES UNDEFINED ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ 1 A recursive module and product system │ │ 2 A typed decision and customization algebra │ │ 3 A typed import/export contract system │ │ 4 An artifact-construction algebra │ │ 5 Read-only concern coverage and obligation audits │ │ 6 A deterministic constraint and conflict solver │ │ 7 A separately staged runtime contract binder │ │ 8 A separately staged physical-provider binder │ │ 9 A completeness and proof system │ └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ Without these, "prebuilt libraries + references + customization" is a slogan. They do not form one compiler: semantic closure, audit and deployment binding have different inputs, authorities and lifecycles.

The superseded weaving model

Weaving is aspect-oriented vocabulary, and aspect systems have a documented failure mode: pointcut fragility — a change in base code silently causes join points to enter or leave an aspect's scope. A large completion matrix is not comprehensible by inspection, and per-cell provenance explains one result without making the whole explicable.

The resolution is stricter than pass discipline. A family or capability emits typed contributions — declare artifact, require contract, offer contract, define or assign a decision, impose a constraint, require an obligation, add a proof obligation, or open a gap — and never obtains mutable access to an application graph. An artifact constructor may derive only artifacts whose identity formula and output kind it owns. Coverage auditors are read-only. Target lowerers own only their target representations. The 24-axis matrix is a derived audit view, not the executable model.

Store artifacts, decisions, contracts, constraints, obligations, derivations, proofs and gaps. Derive coverage. Not the other way round.

0b. Phase 1 — the Constitution Compiler

The missing prerequisite

Phase 1 builds a deterministic typed Constitution Compiler. It compiles the platform's categories, ownership claims, legal arrows, contract modes, decision algebras, laws, proof obligations, mechanisms, provider capabilities and product-layer rules into one immutable ConstitutionEdition. The Constitution Compiler is a platform-control product. It is not a sovereign software-domain family, application compiler, runtime, package manager, provider registry or replacement for native Rust family APIs.

Shared-contract admission is fail-closed

The compiler admits exactly one owner header per contract identity and exact semantic edition. A consumer edge must name its declaring consumer and cite an existing owner header; the owner never authors a mirror export edge. The compiler rejects payload shadows, owner disagreement, illegal artifact-source flow, epistemic strengthening without adjudication, phase inversion, obligation weakening, scope/cardinality incompatibility, missing or ambiguous offers, bare-boolean satisfaction and dependency cycles. 0d.3 · The acceptance-wave defect classes — folded 2026-08-06

The central finding: local specs look complete while cross-family composition fails. A specification describing the right concepts does not yet define one executable, mutually compatible contract. Seven defect classes, each with its kill-rule; the acceptance wave audits every family against all seven before any implementation adoption:

D1 · Seam disagreement. Identifier/Namespace mint-authority ownership; Canonical/Digest prepared-bytes-vs-proof; Instant/Calendar/Bitemporality mapping contracts; Quantity/Rate conversion types; Causality relation-proof vs Bitemporality sequence expectation. Kill-rule: similar names are not compatibility — every real edge resolves to one exact ContractEdgeSpec identity, edition, payload, guarantees, cardinality, phase, witness and refusal ON BOTH SIDES, or is an explicit owned gap. The current generated census is target/architecture/family-seam-catalog.json; mutable counts are evidence, not normative prose. Until every real edge is exact or an owned gap, the DAG is NOT frozen.

D2 · Unimplementable native APIs. Private values without constructors; results without observers; undefined types in public signatures; prose arrows instead of Rust contracts; refusals requiring information absent from inputs; state machines without genesis, admission, or total matrices. Kill-rule: constructor×observer sufficiency + exact-signature manifest closure (§14.1 of the Constitution) run per family; a refusal's evidence must be derivable from the operation's declared inputs.

D3 · Non-executable decisions. Decision values that never reach an operation; missing authority × resolution mode × binding phase; algebras in prose. Kill-rule: every decision row names the exact output/operation it changes (decision-effectiveness trace), and the three algebras are total typed functions, not adjectives.

D4 · Epistemic overreach. Caller-supplied instants treated as "current"; partial history returning NeverAsserted; observations promoted to register truth; local evidence claiming global uniqueness; recall enforced without a disposition witness. Kill-rule: every output carries its epistemic class (mathematical · admitted · claim · observation · adjudicated), and no operation's output class exceeds its inputs' classes without a named adjudication step.

D5 · State/crash/resource incompleteness. Hidden streaming sessions in "stateless" families; obligations losable between prepare and commit; series without genesis or idempotency records; bounds promised but no budget accepted; unbounded vectors in bounded results. Kill-rule: the twelve-generator sweep's partiality/resource rows are mandatory where any session, stream, or obligation exists; every bounded claim names the input that carries the budget.

D6 · Evidence ahead of reality. 16/16 structural checks prove document shape only; tests exercising prior editions prove nothing about this one; first verticals that end inside the family or in fixtures are not composition proof. Kill-rule: proof artifacts name the exact spec edition and digest they exercise; a first vertical must cross at least one real cross-family seam to a consumer that implements the contract.

D7 · Missing implementation supplier. The constitutional metamodel/compiler does not exist; 30/33 candidate packages transitively depend on deprecated suppliers. Kill-rule: no wholesale adoption; salvage only through symbol-level convergence records; the spec compiler is the unblocking dependency for C11 across every row.

0e.1 The one authorable constitutional source

One shape, not one look-alike manifest per family

Every family contributes exactly one ContextSpec using the field vocabulary below. Fields are logical products and may be modules in Rust; they do not imply crates. Conditional products use an empty collection or None with the family’s explicit absence ruling. A family may not rename fields, replace exact members with range shorthand, add a family-local manifest dialect or smuggle a linked library artifact into authored source.

Canonical type is declared once

The exact ContextSpec, PublicItemSpec, InternalModuleSpec, ContractHeader, ContractRequirementSpec, ContractOfferSpec, ContractEdgeSpec and FamilySurfaceEdition definitions live only in the Constitution’s shared-metamodel section. This architecture consumes those types and deliberately does not reproduce them.

Every member is an exact declared identity, never OP-01..OP-20, an unexpanded prose constant, a crate module path or an implementation symbol accidentally standing in for a semantic descriptor. ContextSpec contains no library_ref, implementation identity, provider offer, certificate result, environment binding, secret, runtime observation, generated count or compiled descriptor. The application linker supplies immutable LibraryRefs; the deployment binder supplies certified implementations and providers. The Constitution Compiler validates and projects this source—it does not discover missing meaning from Rust.

Metamodel source identities use the bootstrap grammar <context>.<kind>.<ubiquitous-name>@<edition> with lowercase ASCII kebab segments. They are neither business Identifier-family values nor Rust crate paths. Human row numbers such as OP-01 are aliases only and cannot enter compiled identity.

Cross-family arrows use the Constitution’s one exact ContractHeader and consumer-owned ContractEdgeSpec: identity, exact semantic edition, semantic owner, kind, interaction mode, artifact-source policy, epistemic policy, scope, authority, binding phase, payload type, guarantees, cardinality, applicability, obligations, provenance and lifecycle; plus consumer, cited contract identity, accepted edition requirement, requirement, compatible-offer shape, requirement-join algebra, satisfaction algebra, typed witness and typed refusal. The fourth trace matrix and every owner→consumer export view are generated from those records. They are not prose compatibility tables. A missing owner header remains an explicit consumer gap; the owner never authors a duplicate export edge.

Definition cardFamily-owned immutable semantic data that applications author, select or exchange: for example a digest separation-domain definition or an identifier profile.
Profile cardA named, law-preserving bundle of explicit decisions. A profile may supply values or derivations, but never introduce a behavior branch absent from the native library.
Contribution cardAn authorized candidate assignment with provenance. Several contributions are combined by the decision's own contribution algebra.
Requirement cardA portable typed demand. Several consumers join requirements using the requirement algebra; the result remains unbound until a compatible offer is proven.

Not a cardRuntime observations, secrets, credentials, provider handles, mutable health, generated implementation code, arbitrary JSON bags, builder-only knobs or family-local registry entries.

A family publishes cards only when it owns authorable, selectable or exchangeable data. Otherwise it publishes an explicit empty-card ruling. Card publication is not mandatory anatomy.

0f.0 Agent execution contract

Completion means refined, not merely drafted

The drafting agent performs the protocol after completing the family work, never while using it to invent the family. Its entry criterion is a complete first draft containing the charter, boundary and non-ownership, ontology, laws, native operations and refusals, decision catalog and three algebras, source/card model, exact seams, evolution policy, proof obligations and a proposed physical layout. The agent then executes PDR-01 through PDR-10 in order and appends the required disposition block to the owning family specification. It may not report the family complete, ratifiable or implementation-ready until every pass has an evidenced disposition.

A separate acceptance reviewer verifies the disposition against the repository and the Constitution. The author’s assertion is not acceptance evidence. After implementation, the implementation agent repeats the same protocol with exact symbols, compilation, tests, runnable wiring, provider posture and the first real vertical. A green specification review never upgrades implementation status.

Ground truth is evidence, not supplier authority

Finding an existing crate, compiler, card surface or algorithm prevents a false “missing” claim, but does not make that implementation canonical. The census must classify it under the repository Constitution and choose exactly one disposition: retain as a ratification candidate; salvage named laws, algorithms or vectors through an explicit semantic delta; replace at the proven owner boundary; or tombstone as deprecated inventory. “It exists” does not authorize importing it, while “it is deprecated” does not authorize rebuilding the same responsibility under a new name.

6. The decision algebra

The precise law

"Every lawful variation is data" is directionally right and imprecise. The law is:

Every lawful variation MUST appear as a typed decision in the public decision catalog.

The value may come from an application, policy, reference catalog, deployment binding or certified extension. A runtime observation is evidence, never a decision contribution and never an input to a sealed semantic plan.

7. Merge algebras — the heart of lawful customization

Three algebras — never one generic merge

Contribution merge combines authorized candidate values for one decision. Requirement join combines demands from several consumers into one demand. Offer satisfaction proves that one offer meets one joined requirement. They have different operands, orders, refusals and outputs. Satisfaction returns a typed witness or a typed mismatch, never bool.

contributions ── merge ──▶ resolved decision | decision conflict requirements ── join ──▶ joined requirement | impossible demand offer × joined requirement ── satisfy ──▶ satisfaction witness | mismatch A family owns its typed values and its partial orders. The platform owns the closed vocabulary and orchestration protocol. A string-named "custom algebra" without executable laws is forbidden.

Do not implement overlays with arbitrary precedence and recursive object merging. Different decisions compose differently, and the direction of "stricter" is not a property of the platform — it is a property of the key.
