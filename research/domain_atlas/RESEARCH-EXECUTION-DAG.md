# Research execution DAG

Status: active research program. This is an execution map, not a completeness claim.

## Why a large research budget helps

The useful unit of parallelism is an independently falsifiable universe, not another agent producing
the same list. Every stream must contribute records to the shared graph and must pass through a
different integration and challenge stage before the compiler can depend on it.

## Evidence and promotion gates

```text
hypothesis
    -> sourced candidate
    -> definition/law compared
    -> crosswalk proposed
    -> independent challenge
    -> owner adjudicated
    -> executable conformance oracle
    -> two implementation receipts
    -> compiler-bindable offer
```

No stage may be skipped by renaming a record. String similarity, vendor popularity, a long source
list, and successful schema validation are not evidence of semantic equivalence or runtime
conformance.

## Required output from every research stream

1. Scope, negative scope, neighboring contexts and ownership hypotheses.
2. Machine-readable records with stable IDs, editions and candidate status.
3. Vocabulary, homonyms, invariants, refusals, lifecycle, time and authority semantics.
4. Capability, operation, method, algorithm, kernel and library distinctions where applicable.
5. Requirement/offer/binding implications and every exposed decision point.
6. Primary evidence, claim-level limitations, contradictions and freshness metadata.
7. Non-LLM innovations from 2021 onward, linked to the capabilities they actually change.
8. Unknowns, counterexamples and a generated review queue.
9. Deterministic generation plus structural, reference and schema validators.
10. Promotion tests and at least one reason a plausible-looking mapping must be refused.

For an expert/contributor stream, one expert row is never sufficient. The minimum useful chain is:

```text
person identity -> exact artifact + contribution role -> scoped claim
                -> formal object/method/operation/implementation distinction
                -> evidence + limitation/counterevidence
                -> canonical crosswalk -> compiler requirement/library candidate
```

Authorship, invention, maintenance, advocacy, standardization participation and independent
replication are different relations. Neighboring work may not be assigned to a prominent expert by
topic proximity, citation count or coauthorship.

## Budget allocation rule

Additional research is allocated in this order:

1. close an uncovered plane;
2. deepen a shallow high-risk contract;
3. independently challenge an asserted mapping;
4. test a record in a second unrelated industry;
5. qualify an implementation or provider against an executable oracle;
6. refresh evidence whose edition or operational behavior can change.

It is not allocated to producing synonymous inventories without a mapping, law, decision or proof
that changes the governed graph.
