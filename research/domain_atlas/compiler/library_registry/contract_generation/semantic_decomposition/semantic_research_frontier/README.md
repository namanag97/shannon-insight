# Semantic research frontier

This projection converts the full 16-axis, 10,784-cell applicability surface
into three concurrent work lanes without deleting any family docket, member cell,
candidate cluster or exception:

1. **P0 targeted-evidence rebase** — six axes, 138 family dockets and 4,044
   member-axis cells. The six campaigns route the former 103 evidence-vacancy
   packages/2,805 occurrences; the remaining work is per-member applicability,
   exception research and owner adjudication.
2. **P1 ambiguous-modal split** — four axes, 92 dockets and 2,696 cells. Direct
   structured evidence exists, but seven dockets across semantic object, semantic
   role, effect boundary and representation have no unique modal candidate.
3. **P2 owner-review ready** — six axes, 138 dockets and 4,044 cells. Evidence and
   review candidates exist; broad additional research is not the default next
   operation. Owner challenge, exception review and signed receipts are.

These lanes can execute concurrently because they operate on disjoint axis
dockets. Priority expresses semantic risk and current closure operation, not a
license to propagate conclusions between axes. Shared mechanics are limited to
review protocols, schemas, counterexample search and receipt transport. Meaning,
owners, member applicability, exceptions, contracts and acceptance remain local.

Build and validate:

```text
python3 build_semantic_research_frontier.py
python3 validate.py
```
