# Analytical formalism coverage frontier

This package prevents the semantic-slice program from becoming another 674-library checklist. It
projects every retained product's exact concrete library graph against every live evidence-backed
semantic slice and factors the analytical backlog into 19 formalism quotients.

```text
59 retained products
        |
        +--> exact subject -> concrete-library edges
        |
        +--> intersection with 19 live semantic slices
        |          |
        |          +--> covered concrete refs
        |          +--> uncovered concrete refs
        |          X    overlap != semantic completion
        |
        +--> 19 analytical-formalism clusters
                   |
                   +--> 19 slices encoded, unratified
                   +--> 0 open high-priority quotients
                   +--> 0 open medium-priority quotients
                   +--> 25 typed prerequisite candidates
```

The forecasting/planning cluster is now encoded, but the research rejects that label as a product
collapse. Forecasting Workbench owns future-estimate definition/run/evaluation/governance. A
separate, still-missing `Integrated Planning Workbench` candidate would own scenario, alternative,
feasibility, cross-functional reconciliation, approval, commitment and replanning. Financial,
demand, supply, capacity, inventory and workforce planning remain vertical profiles over that
horizontal planning lifecycle unless further evidence proves separate products.

The ordering is a dependency/fanout research heuristic, not product readiness. The frontier now
separates `MANDATORY_SEMANTIC_FOUNDATION` from `CONDITIONAL_METHOD_IMPORT` and emits the candidate
graph in `formalism-dependency-graph.jsonl`. General statistical inference has the highest
candidate foundation fanout even though the retained notebook product has only one direct concrete
library. Its slice is now encoded with twenty-one justified neighbors and eight typed missing
library seams. This corrects the misleading raw-library ordering: statistical method libraries are
reusable foundations that are currently mostly unconsumed by product graphs.

The query/OLAP/warehouse foundation is also encoded from its exact four-product graph. Its slice
binds all 43 unique declared product libraries plus 20 justified execution and table-read ACL
neighbors. This moves BI/metrics and data preparation from a missing relational prerequisite to a
research-backed but still unratified import surface. It does not make a managed warehouse or
lakehouse experience the owner of query, table, catalog, metric or resource semantics.

The BI/visualization/semantic-metrics quotient is now encoded from its exact three-product graph.
Its slice binds all 39 unique declared product libraries plus 31 justified query, quantity,
provenance, presentation and delivery neighbors. It keeps metric definition, formula evaluation,
metric observation, query result, presentation, report snapshot, alert delivery and business
decision as distinct semantic objects and owner seams. The encoding remains unratified and closes
no owner, applicability, exact-contract or implementation gap.

The quality/reconciliation/controls quotient is encoded from the exact two-product split: 30

requirements, measurements, observations, signals, defects, reconciliation breaks, corrections,

authority, entity merge/split and accounting-control vocabulary remain neighboring or vertical
owners. This encoding is unratified and closes no semantic or implementation gap.

The search/information-retrieval quotient is encoded from the exact twelve-library union declared

Twenty-eight missing-library seams remain unratified.

Priority weights mandatory dependent quotients four times more strongly than conditional imports.
This prevents an optional graph/search relationship from outranking relational/query semantics
that BI and data preparation must either import or explicitly refuse.

A prerequisite edge means that the consumer must import or explicitly refuse the bounded semantic
question; it does not merge contexts, transfer ownership, prove applicability, select a provider or
close a gap. Small direct product graphs such as graph analytics and statistical notebooks may add
many currently unconsumed formalism libraries when their exact universe is derived. Each future
slice must replay declared product edges, add justified formalism neighbors, falsify homonym merges
and keep every owner, applicability, contract and qualification decision unratified.

Twenty-seven retained products are intentionally outside the analytical-formalism cluster list
because they are primarily data movement, runtime, governance, sharing, protection, orchestration,
compiler or commercial/control products. They remain present in the 59-row product coverage tensor
and are not declared complete or irrelevant.
