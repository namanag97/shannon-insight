# Convergence DAG — After Research Resolution

The 625 research-addressable quotients have terminal unratified research decisions. Remaining work is dependency-ordered appraisal and physical evidence, not another 625-row research loop.

```text
CW-01 semantic appraisal/ratification
  -> CW-02 exact contract freeze
      -> CW-03 implementation #1
          -> CW-04 independent qualification
              -> CW-05 second implementation + differential/exit
              -> CW-06 vertical acceptance
                  -> CW-07 product appraisal/ratification
CW-01 -> CW-08 continuous invalidation / standards drift
```
