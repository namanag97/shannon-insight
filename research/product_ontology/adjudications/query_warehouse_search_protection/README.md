# Query, Warehouse, Search and Protection Adjudication

This candidate corpus separates six independently adopted and operated outcome promises while retaining federation, workload profiles, cache, snapshots, replication and vector retrieval at their correct lower boundaries.

```text

            ^                                ^

   snapshot/replication                WORM/object storage
   are mechanisms only                 is a mechanism only
```

Core boundary rulings:

- Federation is a query-execution capability. A separately adopted virtual-relation identity and lifecycle can form the Virtual Data Access product.
- Operational, realtime, interactive and batch are workload or execution profiles, not products by themselves.
- Cache is a cross-product identity, freshness, invalidation and evidence contract; it never owns source truth.
- Vector retrieval belongs to Search and Index Serving. It is not a feature platform, predictive model, decision authority or inherently an AI feature.
- Data Protection and Recovery is distinct from Digital Preservation Archive. Snapshot, replication and immutable storage are mechanisms, not proof of either complete product promise.
- Agents and models are optional intent-selected extensions. Deleting them leaves parsing, typing, constraint solving, planning, authorization, execution, qualification and evidence behavior unchanged.

The generated JSONL views are deterministic projections of `source_model.py`. Every library has a
compiler-registry binding map. All sixty maps now resolve structurally: index mutation generation
and search visibility cut bind the exact persistence contracts alongside virtual-relation
identity/lifecycle, recovery-objective measurement and cache-fill coordination. No implementation
or provider is qualified and no portable offer is claimed.

All six retained products now have complete candidate 29-field DDD dossiers and exact product
attribution for every library and compiler map. The ownership split is 27 query/federation/cache,
4 managed-warehouse experience, 3 virtual-access, 9 search/index, 9 recovery and 8 preservation
contracts. This is product ownership, not provider qualification.

All six products are structurally mapped but unqualified. Search and Index Serving now preserves
the exact chain `source -> projection -> mutation -> acknowledgement -> durability/commit ->
visibility cut -> result cut`; closing its two compiler-contract gaps does not supply an
implementation, qualification receipt or accepted vertical outcome.

Models and agents remain one optional removable extension, not a per-library concern. No AI label
changes query, retrieval, recovery or preservation truth.
