# Global product-boundary research

## Standing

This corpus is a falsifiable research candidate for the horizontal product-boundary universe of
one composable enterprise data and analytics company. It ratifies no product and makes no claim
that the candidate enumeration is complete. The candidate count is an audit fact, not a portfolio
target.

The governing rule is:

```text
Product = defined user and job
        + independently adoptable outcome promise
        + independently operable failure/SLO boundary
        + independently supportable lifecycle
        + billable or allocatable economic unit
        + independently exercisable exit
        + unique semantic and authority boundary
        + stable evidence-bearing interfaces
```

A team, repository, crate, deployment, dashboard, bounded context, capability, vendor SKU, table,
connector, algorithm, standard, provider or control loop does not pass merely by existing. Engines,
control planes, shared services, data products and applications are subjected to the same gates.

## What the corpus establishes

As generated from the edition-1 source corpus:

- 72 horizontal boundary candidates: 48 strong, 11 presumptive, 4 deferred and 9 expected to merge
  or be reclassified.
- 158 reusable obligations/decision points, including all 110 truth dimensions plus adoption,
  operation, support, SLO, security, commercial and exit obligations.
- 7,920 applicability decisions: exactly T001–T110 for every candidate, with state-specific
  reasons, conditions, laws, owners or evidence needs.
- 153 primary or authoritative evidence sources spanning life-cycle and service standards,
  cloud-native contracts, interoperability, data-product/platform practice, official specialist
  documentation and enterprise operating research.
- 25 non-LLM innovations dated 2021–2025 and tied to boundary impact rather than novelty claims.
- All imported context, library and provider records (exact current count in
  `coverage-report.json`), each explicitly marked `not_a_product_by_origin`.
- All upstream artifacts read and content-hashed (exact current count in
  `coverage-report.json`), including every current file under
  `research/domain_atlas/universes/`, every compiler artifact and every composition-pilot artifact,
  plus the global context, truth and pilot product-graph inputs and all twelve evidence-backed
  product-boundary adjudication bundles.
- 21 alternative decompositions, 43 explicit incompatibilities, 36 substitution classes, 9
  industry solution-pack compositions and 20 ratification-blocking gaps.

These counts are regenerated in `coverage-report.json`; validation output is the authoritative
snapshot if upstream artifacts change.

## Six-gate adjudication

The six necessary independence gates are encoded in `boundary-assertions.jsonl`:

1. **Adoptable:** a consumer makes a separable decision, reaches first value and can coexist with a
   predecessor or substitute.
2. **Operable:** health, finite capacity, SLOs, failure, recovery, on-call and evidence are
   attributable to this promise.
3. **Supportable:** versions, severity, response, restoration, escalation and consumer/provider
   responsibilities are published.
4. **Billable or allocatable:** usage and shared cost have stable units and rules even for an
   internal product with no invoice.
5. **Exitable:** configuration, identities, state, data, policies, evidence and in-flight
   obligations can move to a conforming substitute, with deletion/retention disposition.
6. **Semantically and authoritatively coherent:** the boundary owns a unique sovereign question,
   meanings and effects through stable interfaces.

Numeric split scores expose weak evidence; they never override semantic-owner uniqueness. A
candidate that scores highly but duplicates a metric, identity, policy, dataset or authority owner
must be narrowed, translated or refused.

## Refused plausible bundles

The corpus explicitly refuses several tempting decompositions:

- **One data platform product:** source authority, movement, table state, catalog authority, query,
  metrics, policy, evidence, consumption and operations do not share one semantic, failure or exit
  boundary.
- **One lakehouse product:** table formats are standards/semantic contracts/capabilities, while
  catalog/commit, query, managed maintenance, sharing and managed experience have separately
  adjudicated product boundaries; a suite may package them without re-owning their meanings.
- **One governance product:** glossary, master/reference data, policy decisions, lineage evidence,
  data fitness, privacy cases and discovery projections cannot share authority without conflicts.
- **Query engine owns metrics:** physical planning cannot own business population, grain, measure or
  join semantics.
- **One AI/ML platform:** predictive-method validity, model engineering/lifecycle, point-in-time
  feature serving, inference SLOs, model assurance, deterministic decision execution and optional
  generated-proposal runtimes have distinct meanings, authorities and exits. Vector retrieval stays
  with search/index serving rather than feature semantics.
- **One product per analytical label:** a practice such as statistics, causal inference, anomaly
  detection, graph analysis, text analysis or signal analysis becomes a product only if a separate
  user/job, adoption, lifecycle, operation, support, economics and exit promise is proven.
- **AI prefix everywhere:** deterministic algorithms remain first-class. Models, LLMs and agents
  bind only through prohibited, optional or intent-required ports and gain no semantic or effect
  authority from the modality label.
- **Product per connector, codec, table, context, crate, chart or industry:** these are provider,
  implementation, asset, semantic or composition identities unless separately proven through the
  six gates.

Each refusal has a reopening condition. New evidence can change the adjudication; category labels
cannot.

## Industry solution packs

An industry pack owns vertical vocabulary, analytical cases, population and grain, local rules,
decision authority, source mappings and acceptance evidence. It separately declares the
horizontal products it composes and the provider-neutral analytical method contracts it requires.
The included examples cover built/food environments, commerce,
energy, finance, health, manufacturing, public/education, telecom/media/technology and
transport/logistics.

The falsifier is direct: if moving from, for example, acute-care deterioration to supply-network
constraint localization requires the forecasting, process-mining, policy or publication product to
rename or fork its horizontal owned meanings, the horizontal boundary is wrong. The pack may add
an anti-corruption mapping or a vertical context; it may not manufacture an industry-specific
horizontal engine.

## Applicability, not blind duplication

Every candidate has one record for every truth dimension, but applicability is computed from the
candidate posture:

- user-interface duties are conditional for API/embedded engines;
- data contracts, quality and time are conditional where no enterprise data is owned;
- model lifecycle is inapplicable for non-model products;
- disaster recovery is inapplicable to a pure/stateless boundary while correctness and artifact
  retention remain required elsewhere;
- Rust/build truths are conditional on a Rust implementation rather than treated as universal
  product properties;
- live-source migration and independent appraisal remain undetermined until an implementation and
  reviewer exist; and
- compatibility aliases that duplicate obsolete names or owners are prohibited for product
  candidates that survive the boundary test.

Silence is never valid. `REQUIRED`, `CONDITIONAL`, `PROHIBITED`, `INAPPLICABLE` and `UNDETERMINED`
each carry the field needed to make the decision falsifiable.

## Evidence posture

The source corpus favors primary standards and first-party specifications/documentation. Examples
include [ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288](https://www.iso.org/standard/81702.html),
[ISO/IEC 20000-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/70636.html),
[NIST SP 800-53](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final),
[Kubernetes controllers](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/architecture/controller/),
[OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/),
[Apache Iceberg](https://iceberg.apache.org/spec/),
[Iceberg REST](https://iceberg.apache.org/rest-catalog-spec/),
[Substrait](https://substrait.io/spec/specification/),
[OpenLineage](https://openlineage.io/docs/spec/),
[W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/), and practitioner evidence on
[designing data products](https://martinfowler.com/articles/designing-data-products.html).

Official specialist documentation is used as implementation and substitution evidence, not as
permission to copy vendor packaging. A documentation page proves only the feature, protocol,
limit or exit mechanism stated in its evidence record.

## `cannot_ratify_until`

Ratification is blocked until the gaps in `gaps.jsonl` close, including:

- the missing global context map and 559 distinct unadjudicated context candidates;
- semantic-owner uniqueness across metric, dataset, identity, policy and authority;
- independent implementations, law oracles, cycle/removal analysis and target qualification for
  library boundaries;
- deployed provider/source occurrences with versions, regions, limits, cost and fresh probes;
- executable compiler stages, typed operation totality, semantic diff and binding invalidation;
- cross-provider SLO/capacity/security evidence and complete supplier-exit drills;
- an independently appraised two-release vertical; and
- end-to-end proof that two unrelated industries compile without horizontal name or meaning
  branches.

Passing this corpus validator proves structure, references, thresholds, applicability coverage,
upstream digest freshness and deterministic generation only. It does not prove product-market fit,
production readiness, legal sufficiency, security certification, provider equivalence or portfolio
completion.
