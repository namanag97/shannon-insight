# Platform and runtime-control product adjudication

Status: evidence-backed adjudicated candidate, not ratified products or qualified providers.

```text
composable data-platform pattern
        |
        v
data and analytics platform suite ---------------- packaging only
        |
        +--> intent-to-solution compiler ---------- presumptive product
        +--> data-product developer platform ------ strong product candidate
        +--> runtime resource control plane ------- strong product candidate
        `--> usage/cost/FinOps allocation --------- strong product candidate

provider qualification broker ------------------- compiler assurance subsystem
platform operations ------------------------------ split composition, not one product
```

All four products now have complete 29-field DDDs. The solution compiler has eleven attributed library
contracts spanning canonical-reference resolution, staged semantic IR/lowering, model-class
adjudication, binding/constraint evidence, conformance coordination, target-artifact planning,
release governance and incremental trace/replay. Every compiler map names both the exact external
library or toolchain boundary and its owning registry. This is structural closure, not evidence
that the presumptive product boundary is independently adopted, qualified or accepted.

The developer platform owns four blueprint, paved-path planning/runtime and platform-conformance
seams. A portal, blueprint, template, instantiation plan, effect occurrence, conformant instance and
accepted service remain distinct.

The runtime control plane owns 22 product-facing seams and keeps work, resource demand, offer,
compatibility, quota, admission, scheduling, placement, reservation, allocation, lease/fencing,
preemption, autoscaling, backpressure, cancellation, checkpoint, backend, attempt, receipt, usage,
energy and conformance identities distinct. The former collapsed allocation/lease facade has been
removed. FinOps owns eight seams and keeps runtime usage, cost occurrence, normalized cost,
attribution, allocated cost, reconciled residual, unit cost, budget/precharge, charge and invoice
distinct. The compiler keeps intent, candidate plan, binding, qualification, authorized effect and
accepted outcome apart.

Fifty local library contracts have fifty compiler maps: forty-five are attributed to the four
products and five remain explicit cross-cutting/component seams. All fifty are unqualified
structural projections and no structural binding gap remains. The FinOps product's normalization
seam binds an exact, editioned FOCUS contract with residual conservation and typed loss/refusal
behavior. The former generic telemetry facade now projects to ten independently owned contracts:
attribution, schema conventions, traces, metrics, logs/events, profiles, propagation,
observation reduction/loss, cross-signal correlation and export delivery. Profiles remain alpha;
all implementations and providers remain unqualified and non-portable.

Agents and models remain optional proposal sources. They cannot type intent, prove compatibility,
qualify providers, admit/allocate resources, authorize effects, determine cost truth or issue
receipts.
