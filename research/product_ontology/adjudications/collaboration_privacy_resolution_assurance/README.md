# Collaboration, privacy, entity-resolution and assurance adjudication

Status: evidence-backed candidate boundaries; not ratified, qualified, complete, or legal advice.

This pass closes four overloaded governance/trust labels into four independently adopted product
promises. It also reclassifies the “all governance” label as a suite.


The central non-collapses are:

- a clean-room product promise is not a warehouse, TEE, MPC protocol, DP mechanism or agent;
- request authentication is not subject scope, authority, effect or verified disposition;
- a match score is not a link decision, entity cluster, golden record or source mutation;
- a test result or signed attestation is not independent appraisal or a relying decision; and
- “independent” is evidenced for an appraisal occurrence, never inherited from software or a brand.

Predictive models remain valid analytical-method implementations for entity scoring. LLMs and
tool-using agents may be selected for drafting, navigation, triage or explanation. They remain
removable and non-authoritative: the deterministic contracts still parse, type, solve constraints,
run numerical methods, qualify providers, enforce policy, execute effects and emit receipts.

All four products now have complete product-specific 29-field DDD dossiers. Controlled collaboration
owns the participant/contribution/approved-analysis/output-release chain without absorbing PETs.
Privacy owns the evidence-bearing case/effect/appeal/recall lifecycle without interpreting law.
Entity resolution owns score-to-link-to-cluster decisions without source or master mutation.
Assurance owns claim-argument-evidence appraisal and bounded verdict lifecycle without making the
relying decision.

Generated contents include product DDD surfaces, semantic owners, capability/library contracts,
requirements, unqualified provider offers, exact projections to the compiler library registry,
negative twins and explicit semantic/conformance gaps.
