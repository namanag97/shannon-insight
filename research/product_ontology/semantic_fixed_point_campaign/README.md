# Semantic fixed-point closure campaign

## Purpose

Close every research-addressable semantic gap as far as research can legitimately close it, while
recursively discovering and resolving new semantic gaps introduced by stronger evidence or changed
boundaries. This package does **not** manufacture project ratification, implementation, qualification,
physical execution, independent appraisal or vertical acceptance.

As of 2026-08-27:

- Starting research frontier: **625 quotients / 14,496 atoms**.
- P01 source-family authority decisions audited: **23 / 23**.
- Newly discovered semantic gaps during stricter research: **9**.
- Newly discovered gaps with evidence-bounded effective resolutions: **9 / 9**.
- P02-P05 detailed records revalidated: **14,473**.
  - semantic migrations: **6,481**;
  - authority reroutes: **7,992**;
  - unchanged: **0**.
- Effective known research vacancies: **0**.
- Canonical gaps closed by this campaign: **0**.
- Ratified decisions invented: **0**.
- Implementations, qualifications and vertical acceptances invented: **0**.

The fixed-point result therefore means:

> All currently known research-addressable quotients and every semantic delta discovered by this
> campaign have an evidence-bounded **proposed, unratified** resolution. It does not mean the
> proposals have become project law or that physical/product qualification gates are closed.

## Major corrections produced by the campaign

The prior convergence packet treated several heterogeneous packages as if one source could provide
family-wide semantic authority. The fixed-point audit replaces that model with explicit authority
routing.

### Candidate data shapes

There is no universal data-shape semantic authority. The 33 members now route to native authorities
or explicit bounded owners, including IFC, DICOM, FHIR, ISO 20022, FIX, FITS, HDF5, netCDF/CF,
GA4GH, STAC/DCAT, RFC mail/calendar protocols, JOSE/COSE, glTF and engineering/geospatial profiles.

### Geospatial analytics

CRS, feature/geometry, raster/grid, moving-feature/trajectory, point-cloud representation and
analytical/publication semantics are distinct. OGC/ISO representation standards do not become
application-decision authorities.

### Operations research

Operations Research is a formalism portfolio. Decision/objective authority, exact optimization,
heuristics, queueing and simulation retain separate assumptions, evidence strengths and result
semantics. A feasible or optimized result is not an authorized business action.

### Forecasting lifecycle

Forecast definition, temporal/information semantics, estimator/evaluation methods, immutable
forecast editions, selection/appraisal, human override and publication are separate layers. A score,
rank or forecast is not a plan, commitment, authorization or outcome.

### Shared semantic foundations

"Shared" no longer means "put reusable-looking domain policy here". The campaign re-homes:

- business-calendar policy out of generic time primitives;
- monetary semantics out of physical quantity ownership;
- probability semantics into statistical/mathematical foundations;
- verification receipts out of intent and into evidence/assurance foundations.

### Platform commercial/support

Tenant and platform-account identity are re-homed to Platform Estate & Tenancy Administration.
Commercial-customer roles import upstream party/legal-entity identity. Supplier exit and
service-maintenance are imported from their own lifecycle owners rather than silently owned by a
commercial mega-aggregate.

### Provenance, metadata and visualization

- W3C PROV propositions, operational lineage observations, authenticated attestations, verification,
  appraisal and acceptance are distinct epistemic layers.
- SKOS concept schemes, OWL ontologies, ISO/IEC 11179 metadata registries, DCAT catalogs and
  master/code/certification lifecycles remain distinct.
- visualization grammar, accessibility, semantic metrics/query meaning, disclosure, publication and
  interaction state are separate authorities.

## Fixed-point law

A research resolution is never terminal merely because a prior generator called it terminal.
Every effective decision is reopenable when stronger evidence, an authority change or a material
counterexample invalidates its bounded assumptions.

The campaign cycle is:

```text
research evidence
  -> bounded proposed ruling
  -> counterexample / non-collapse audit
  -> ownership and dependency diff
  -> downstream P02-P05 revalidation
  -> discover semantic residuals
  -> resolve or reopen
  -> repeat until no known research vacancy remains
```

## Generated outputs

## Validation

Run:

```text
python3 research/product_ontology/semantic_fixed_point_campaign/build_fixed_point.py
python3 research/product_ontology/semantic_fixed_point_campaign/validate_fixed_point.py
```

The branch workflow `.github/workflows/semantic-fixed-point.yml` executes both and persists the
generated projection. The validator fails closed if the starting 625/14,496 frontier drifts without
an explicit rebase, any newly discovered research gap lacks an effective resolution, P01 ceases to
cover exactly 23 families, or any projection invents completion, ratification, implementation,
qualification or vertical-acceptance evidence.

## What remains outside research closure

The research fixed point intentionally leaves these gates open:

1. named project semantic-authority ratification;
2. independent verification of authority receipts;
3. implementation identities and executable code;
4. reproducible-build and law-oracle execution evidence;
5. independent implementations where portability is claimed;
6. provider/target qualification and physical binding;
7. executed unrelated-vertical acceptance and product qualification.

Those are real work, but they cannot be closed by adding more research prose or by relabeling a
proposal as evidence.
