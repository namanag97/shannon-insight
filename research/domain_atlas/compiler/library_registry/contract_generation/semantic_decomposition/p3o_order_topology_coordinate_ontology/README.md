# P3O order/topology coordinate ontology and member rebase

This package replaces the idea that a library has one `Order`, `Hierarchy` or
`Graph` profile. Order and topology belong to an identified endpoint domain,
relation edition, scope and use site.

Twenty relation archetypes distinguish sequence, total and partial orders,
preorders, equivalence classes, causal relations, dependency DAGs,
partition-local logs, taxonomy, partonomy, multigraphs, hypergraphs, spatial and
temporal topology, ranking, render order, join paths, provenance influence and
allocation/exclusion topology.

The relation coordinate records endpoint identity and type, arity, direction,
multiplicity, algebraic laws, comparability outcomes, partition scope, tie and
null policy, paths, cycles, witnesses and representation status. Thirty-two
kernels factor recurring relation transformations without asserting their
applicability.

The old `total_order`, `partial_order`, `hierarchy`, `graph_topology` and
`spatial_topology` facets remain lossy lexical discovery projections. They
cannot establish a relation, scope, causal witness, member applicability or
exact contract.

All 623 P3O target members are preserved exactly in
`member-relation-routes.jsonl` and partition losslessly into 58 research
clusters. No owner, relation profile, applicability or canonical gap is
decided.
