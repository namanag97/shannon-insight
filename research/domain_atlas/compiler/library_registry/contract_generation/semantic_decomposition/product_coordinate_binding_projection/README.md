# Product Coordinate Binding Projection

This package lifts exact library-level semantic-coordinate obligations into the existing
product and composition graph. It is a projection, not evidence and not a completion claim.

The join is identifier-exact:

1. 470 product qualification subjects declare explicit concrete-library references.
2. Canonical P5 gap libraries bind to their 16-axis coordinate dockets.
3. Other references retain their explicit P6 resolution class; they are never joined by name.
4. Subject refusals propagate to 59 retained product assembly dockets.
5. Capability imports, nine researched industry solution packs and four structural vertical
   compositions preserve unresolved product/library boundaries and refuse compilation.

The 39 capability imports and one solution-pack edge that still name merge/defer product
candidates link to exact `boundary_migration_projection` dockets. Their replacement target sets
remain explicit and unselected until responsibility-level migration and owner ratification occur.

`compiler-assembly-gates.jsonl` is the common fail-closed surface for product, solution-pack
and vertical composition assembly. A structural map cannot substitute for semantic authority,
an exact contract, a qualified implementation, a portable offer, or executed vertical acceptance.

Rebuild and validate with:

```sh
python3 build_product_coordinate_binding_projection.py
python3 validate.py
```
