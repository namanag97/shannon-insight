# Retained-product DDD and compiler readiness

This derived matrix prevents product-boundary coverage from being confused with implementation
readiness. It evaluates every strong or presumptive candidate against separate proof stages:

1. exact boundary adjudication;
2. the global 110-truth applicability profile;
3. a product-specific 29-field strategic/tactical DDD dossier;
4. exact product-to-library attribution;
5. total coverage of each internally owned product capability by an attributed owning library,
   while keeping imported product/provider/resource requirements separate;
6. exact product-library-to-compiler maps or typed blocking gaps;
7. current provider qualification and portability evidence; and
8. structural and executed proof in unrelated enterprise verticals.

`UNDETERMINED_PRODUCT_ATTRIBUTION` does not say that a bundle has no useful libraries. It says the
current records do not prove which exact libraries completely and non-overlappingly implement that
specific product. Likewise, `STRUCTURAL_ONLY` vertical coverage is not domain acceptance.

Models and agents cannot close a readiness stage by assertion. They may help research or propose
typed artifacts, but vocabulary, invariants, lifecycle, mappings, conformance, qualification,
authority, effects, receipts and acceptance remain governed evidence.

Current finite-edition standing: 66 retained products, 66 complete candidate DDD dossiers, 66
products with explicit product-library/compiler maps, all 66 structurally mapped but unqualified,
zero mapped-but-blocked products, zero undetermined attributions or uncovered internally owned
capabilities, 186 physical/acceptance closure items, and zero qualified, portable, accepted or
build-ready products.
