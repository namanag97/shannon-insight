# P1 source authority and public-symbol adjudication

This program begins semantic closure only after all 23 source families pass structural controls. It creates exact-digest source-authority decision packets and ranks every repeated public symbol by cross-family fanout and definition conflict.

The output deliberately makes no authority or ownership decision. Equal names and identical placeholder digests are routing evidence only. Shared-owner hypotheses require semantic definitions, identity/equality/lifecycle laws, negative homonym twins and a named owner decision.

`high-fanout-semantic-research.jsonl` records bounded primary research for the currently selected
collision seams. It distinguishes a scoped shared carrier candidate from a qualified family profile
or a true homonym; its dispositions remain unratified. In particular, the program refuses to
collapse lease request/grant/renewal/revocation, cancellation intent/notification/cessation, or
transformation/provenance/loss/acceptance into single states.

The researched capability-port triad additionally separates a consumer requirement, provider
offer, structural import/export, candidate binding, conformance-oracle result and independent
qualification. These distinctions project to all 27 representation/codec libraries for each of
the three shared traits.

The experiment-cut module separates eligibility, realized assignment, actual and counterfactual
exposure, metric definition, metric observation, data snapshot/watermark, interim-look occurrence,
statistical stopping eligibility and operational stop authority. `AssignmentCut`, `ExposureCut`
and `MetricCut` are shared experiment-domain carrier candidates projected into the analysis-binding
compiler and the analysis-cut/stopping method kernel with different local residuals. Neither
library may treat the availability of a reproducible cut as permission to inspect, stop, ship or
deploy.

The quality/reconciliation family now imports four governed carrier candidates rather than repeating
unqualified placeholders. `QualityEvaluationScope` binds the exact subject snapshot, target or
selection predicate, grain, temporal/data cut, policy/metric editions, exclusions and unknown
coverage. `QualityPolicyEdition` binds authority, policy identity/profile, immutable semantic
content, dependencies, precedence/defaults and lifecycle. The 37 occurrences of each receive
library-local profiles; evaluation scope is not a result or authorization scope, and policy edition
is not a bundle revision, activation state, decision or enforcement receipt.
`QualityEvaluationEvidenceRecord` binds the exact evaluation occurrence, scoped result, witnesses,
coverage and unknowns, provenance, integrity and supersession. It is not an audit occurrence, SCITT
ledger receipt, certificate or acceptance decision. `QualityOperationRefusal` keeps invalid request,
cancellation, resource/provider failure and unknown completion separate from successful negative
outcomes such as nonconformance, anomaly, unmatched reconciliation or a denied, not-applicable or
indeterminate decision. HTTP and telemetry errors remain border mappings, not the domain refusal.

The process/graph batch separates three commonly collapsed artifacts. `TypedGraphView` is an exact
projection contract over a graph edition; it is not the source graph, an event knowledge graph by
default, a path result or a process model. `EventLogView` binds event identity, inclusion, activity
classification, object/case correlation, ordering, temporal cut, state reconstruction, completeness
and projection loss. XES, OCEL/OCED, state-aware OCEL and temporal event-knowledge-graph views are
profiles, not interchangeable encodings. `QualifiedProcessModel` is only a formalism-qualified
envelope: Petri nets, object-centric Petri nets, process trees, declarative models, directly-follows
graphs and graph aggregations retain their own behavioral semantics. A discovered model is not a
normative model, and fitness is not correctness.

`symbol-occurrence-applicability.jsonl` projects every researched proposition back onto every exact
library occurrence. Each row names the candidate qualified profile, candidate public name and local
residual requirements. No missing row means “not applicable,” and no candidate becomes an import or
rename until both the shared proposition and the local residual are ratified.

`symbol-research-archetype-ontology.json` and `remaining-symbol-research-batches.jsonl` route every
non-high-fanout symbol into a bounded research and owner-adjudication batch. The archetypes concern policy/scope, evidence and
receipts, failure, identity, lifecycle, resources, representation, topology, authority, measurement,
capability ports and operations. This is scheduling metadata only: lexical classification neither
establishes semantic equality nor licenses one batch conclusion to be copied to its members.

`archetype-research-programs.jsonl` is the next coordination layer. It schedules the 89 batches as
17 active archetype programs and projects each program onto the applicable subset of the 18 governed
research archetypes and 16 semantic axes, producing 157 reusable research lanes. Questions, primary-source searches and
counterexample methods can be reused within a lane; semantic conclusions cannot. Every one of the
191 symbol packets and every exact occurrence still requires its own disposition, applicability and
named owner decision.

The first full archetype lane is `OPERATION_BOUNDARY_AND_EFFECT`. Bounded comparison of HTTP method
properties, OData actions/functions, GraphQL query/mutation/subscription execution, gRPC
cancellation, reliable-message delivery and PROV activity/evidence semantics yields a candidate
operation-contract kernel. `operation-contract-classification-candidates.jsonl` projects it across
all 73 remaining operation symbols and 152 occurrences. The projection distinguishes describe/read,
declare/bind, validate/appraise, derive/transform, plan/prepare, request/submit, running-occurrence
control, mutation, authority action, append/record, stream/subscription, dispatch and local
materialization roles. These are research routes only: safety, effects, atomicity, idempotency,
cancellation, delivery and completion remain unresolved per symbol and occurrence.

The former general-owner catch-all has been eliminated from the current queue. Forty-seven symbols
covering 98 occurrences now route to explicit domain-contract/adapter, activity-event/audit,
cryptographic-suite/period, capability-port, policy, evidence, identity, shape, authority or
measurement archetypes. Two particularly misleading commercial contract names—payment collection
and source finality—were also pulled out of lexical shape/time buckets. The three new archetypes have
bounded primary research and non-collapse laws; all 47 rows remain unratified routing candidates.

The capability/conformance lane is now separated from its Rust carrier syntax. Twenty symbols
covering 40 exact occurrences are projected into provider-independent analytical, compute, storage,
preparation, transformation and conformance-check ports; backend provider ports; provider offers;
conformance evaluation results; and evidence receipts. Its lifecycle keeps semantic port definition,
consumer requirement, provider offer, satisfaction result, selected binding, implementation artifact,
conformance result, evidence receipt, independent qualification, runtime invocation and current
availability distinct. A language trait is not a semantic capability; signature is not behavior;
offer is not availability; match is not binding; conformance is not interoperability, fitness or
qualification; one implementation is not portability; and an adapter is not the semantic owner.
All classifications remain unratified research routes.

The policy/scope/profile/edition lane is also routed by semantic role instead of policy-shaped
words. Twenty-four original policy-shaped symbols covering 52 occurrences were re-examined, and the
measurement-lane audit additionally moved `QualityRule` here. Eighteen symbols covering 40
occurrences remain as analysis-plan bindings and editions, profile specifications and editions,
decision and appraisal policies, policy compositions, applicability scopes, lifecycle rules,
profile contract ports, scheduling strategies and quality-evaluation rule specifications. The other seven route to evidence/results,
identity, representation, time/lifecycle, authority and shape archetypes. The policy kernel keeps
definition, immutable edition, applicability scope, evaluation input, evaluation occurrence and
result, obligation/advice, enforcement intent, activation, effect receipt and decision log distinct.
In particular, applicability is not permit; obligation is not fulfillment; publication is not
activation; estimand is not estimate; and scheduler selection is not resource binding.

The evidence lane now distinguishes claim, evidence item or bundle, cryptographic proof, evaluation
occurrence, scoped result, execution receipt, transparency receipt, appraisal verdict, policy
decision, certificate and relying-party acceptance. Nineteen symbols covering 38 occurrences were
re-routed: six symbols and 12 occurrences remain evidence/receipt/appraisal records. Prospective
experiment check sets move to policy, codec results to representation, predicate type to identity,
compatibility result to capability satisfaction, and five symbols covering eleven occurrences move
to a new `ANALYTICAL_METHOD_RESULT_AND_DIAGNOSTIC` archetype. That archetype keeps identified
estimand, effect estimate, prediction, comparison, solver/formula outcome, diagnostic, sensitivity,
termination, receipt, conclusion and action authority distinct.

The identity/reference/version/digest lane now has bounded primary research and an exact projection
over all eleven remaining symbols and twenty-two occurrences. It keeps semantic subject, identifier,
reference, locator, resolver result, alias assertion, immutable edition, version relation, canonical
representation and digest evidence separate. UUID uniqueness is not business identity; digest
equality is not semantic equality; version precedence is not compatibility or activation; actor
identity is not authority; and runtime attempt identity is not job, trace, idempotency or effect
identity. Most duplicate occurrences remain family-owner/import hypotheses. One apparent cross-family
reuse is explicitly rejected: process-event `ObjectIdentity` and storage `ObjectIdentity` require
qualified local identities because their namespaces, lifecycle and equality relations differ.
Forecast and spatial `PublicationProfileId` remain a shared-owner hypothesis with local refinements,
not an inferred import.

The authority/security/credential lane now classifies ten symbols and twenty occurrences without
turning identity into permission. Assurance level is function- and risk-profile qualified;
credential, principal, authentication and authorization remain separate; policy obligation is not
fulfillment; a provider key or secret handle is not stable key/secret identity; tenant scope is not
an isolation boundary; and a declared isolation class is not deployed-control evidence. The
cross-family `FencingToken` is a shared-owner hypothesis with an asymmetric seam: runtime/resource
control is the candidate issuer/order owner, while persistence cache-fill coordination is a
consumer that must prove every protected effect sink rejects stale tokens. Token monotonicity alone
does not prove lock ownership, effect exclusion or commit success.

The representation/codec/schema/layout lane is routed by semantic object rather than substring.
`ForecastOrigin` and `ForecastHorizon` are temporal semantics, while `FittedForecaster` is a
lifecycle-bearing analytical-model artifact. The seven genuine representation symbols cover fourteen
occurrences: document-derived views, codec execution outcomes, profiled frames, formula syntax trees,
manifests and policy request attribute bags. Decode success is not application or domain acceptance; encode
success is not losslessness or canonicality; AST equality is not formula-semantic equivalence;
OCR/layout views are derived evidence rather than source truth; and an attribute bag is not a policy
or decision. `Manifest` is explicitly a qualified homonym: a research-object metadata graph and a
storage snapshot membership graph require distinct public identities. Generic frame mechanics may
be shared, but protocol payload type, flags, stream association, limits and state transitions remain
editioned profile semantics.

The resource/bound/capacity/scheduling lane is now separated from words that merely sound physical.
`Block` and `Page` move to shape/topology, `Bound` moves to measurement and `Frame` moves to
representation. The three genuine resource symbols cover six occurrences: reservation ports, buffers
and finite analytical budgets. Memory-runtime reserve and durable reservation-ledger reserve require
qualified local symbols because their pools, identities, lifecycles and receipts differ. Queue-model
`Buffer` and columnar-layout `Buffer` are also qualified homonyms: logical waiting capacity with
discipline/overflow is not a typed physical memory region. `ResourceBudget` remains a family-shared
candidate between analysis binding and conclusion appraisal, with occurrence-specific dimensions,
accounting, exhaustion and partial-result profiles. Request is not reservation; reservation is not
allocation; limit is not guarantee; release is not reclaimed capacity; and budget declaration is not
enforcement.

The shape/topology/view/process lane now classifies seven symbols and fifteen occurrences. Four are
qualified homonyms: graph partition is not runtime exchange partition; document/derived-layout
blocks are not experimental-design blocks; document pages are not Parquet column-chunk pages; and a
document-analysis layout profile is not a physical data-layout/access-path profile. Geometry,
document region and spatial weights remain family-shared owner/import candidates with distinct
occurrence profiles. Geometry binds its CRS, axis order, units, dimensionality, precision and
topology model; a spatial-weight graph additionally binds observation order, neighbor construction,
directedness, transformation and isolate policy. Equal members or equal placeholder digests do not
establish equal topology, objective, identity or ownership.

`measure-lane-archetype-refinements.jsonl` records the six-symbol routing correction, while
`measure-contract-classification-candidates.jsonl` contains the three genuine measure contracts
across six occurrences.
`DimensionAlgebraContract` and `DimensionAlgebraInput` are canonical shared-owner/import candidates:
the shared quantity foundation is the candidate owner and the semantic-formula family is an editioned
consumer. Their contract separates quantity, quantity kind, dimension, unit, numeric value,
commensurability, conversion, uncertainty, syntax and evaluation outcome. `Bound` is a qualified
homonym: an optimization primal/dual objective-bound witness is not a Parquet/file-statistics
lower/upper pruning bound. Objective sense, feasibility, incumbent, gap and solver tolerance qualify
the first; physical/logical type, sort order, truncation, nulls, NaNs and tight/conservative semantics
qualify the second. Neither bound proves row-level truth, an incumbent, feasibility or optimality.
The former measurement bucket also routed `EffectEstimate` to analytical results, `QualityRule` to
policy specifications and `BaselineArtifact` to the analytical-model lifecycle kernel.

The time/lifecycle kernel classifies five symbols across ten occurrences. `ForecastOrigin` and
`ForecastHorizon` are shared forecasting-time semantics imported by estimator and method libraries;
origin, cutoff, event time and issue time remain distinct. `DispositionDue` is an eligibility result
owned by retention calculus and imported by a deletion provider; due does not authorize or prove
deletion. Audit `EventTime` is distinct from observed, ingested, recorded and commit time.
`Retraction` is a qualified homonym: withdrawing reliance on a governed record or assertion is not a
negative materialized-relation update. All clock, occurrence, authority, transition, propagation and
effect claims remain explicit.

The failure/refusal kernel classifies three symbols across six occurrences. Invalid input, semantic
undefinedness, unsupported capability, not-applicable evaluation, negative domain result, refusal,
cancellation, deadline, resource exhaustion, provider failure, partial output and unknown completion
remain distinct. `CanonicalizationError` uses a shared envelope with explicit profile imports;
`DimensionalAlgebraError` belongs to the quantity algebra and is imported by semantic formulas; and
forecast/spatial publication-profile refusals share an outer carrier only through domain-qualified
variants. Retryable is not idempotent, cancellation is not cessation and a transport problem is not
the domain failure.

The analytical-model artifact kernel closes the final primary-research archetype. A model
specification, fitting occurrence, fitted artifact edition, evaluation, selection, approval,
deployment, active serving instance, prediction, monitoring result and retirement are different
objects and states. `BaselineArtifact` is a versioned reference-model artifact owned provisionally by
the anomaly-baseline library and imported by anomaly detectors; it is not a current observation,
threshold, alert or anomaly judgment. `FittedForecaster` is provisionally owned by forecast
estimators and imported by forecasting methods; fitted is not evaluated, selected, approved,
deployed or fit for use. Refit/recomputation creates a new semantic artifact edition, while a content
digest identifies an exact representation without proving semantic equivalence or portability.

The current deterministic cut has bounded primary research for all 210 repeated public symbols:
19 high-fanout propositions plus 191 symbols factored through seventeen reusable archetype kernels.
The 191 non-high-fanout packets remain routed into 89 owner-shaped batches and 17 active archetype
programs across 157 semantic-axis lanes because research does not ratify an owner, shared import,
qualified homonym, occurrence applicability or migration. There are no remaining primary-research
archetypes, but all 210 symbols—including the 19 directly researched high-fanout packets—and all 666
exact occurrences remain owner/applicability-unratified. Zero canonical exact gaps close through
research alone.
